Financial Literacy Classroom

Learn important financial concepts through simple lessons.

01. What is a Bank?

A bank is a financial institution that accepts deposits, provides loans and offers payment services.

02. What is Saving?

Saving means keeping a part of your available money for future needs.

03. What is Interest?

Interest is the amount paid for borrowing money or earned on certain deposits.

04. What is UPI?

UPI is a digital payment system that supports bank-to-bank payments.

05. What is Insurance?

Insurance helps protect against specified financial risks under a policy.

06. Cyber Safety

Never share OTP, PIN, passwords or confidential banking information with anyone.

Pension Information Centre

Learn about pension services and safe information practices.

What is Pension?

A pension is a regular payment made to eligible people under a pension scheme. Eligibility, amount and payment procedures depend on the scheme.

Important Safety Tips

  • Use official government or bank channels.
  • Do not share OTP or PIN with strangers.
  • Verify pension information through authorised offices.
  • Keep required documents safe.
